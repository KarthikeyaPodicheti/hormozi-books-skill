# Chapter 11 — Core Four On Steroids: More Better New

## Core Idea
When leads stall there are only three moves: do **More** of what works, do it **Better**, or do it somewhere **New** — in that order. Nobody saturates a market as early as they think (Size-of-the-Pie Fallacy).

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Rule of 100**: 100 primary advertising actions per day, 100 days straight, per channel (100 reach-outs/day; 100 min/day content; 100 min/day ads).
- **Constraint testing** ("Better"): find the step with the biggest lead drop-off; improving the constraint by 5% can double output while other steps move ~10–16%. One test/week/platform; log results; abandon after 4 failed tries (a month).
- **New, ordered by ROI**: new placement on current platform → known placements on new platform → new core-four activity.

## Key Concepts
- More comes first: doubling inputs doubles engaged leads with zero new skill.
- The diagnostic question: "What's stopping this business from doing ten times what it's doing now?" Sometimes nothing — just go.
- Every action before purchase is a potential drop-off; measure each step (e.g., 30% optin / 5% apply / 50% schedule).
- All core-four combinations boost each other — methods compound.

## Anti-patterns
- Declaring saturation on one platform at a high monthly ad budgetnth against a a large industry.
- Random testing (multiple changes at once = learning nothing); wasting tests on cosmetic tweaks.
- Jumping to "new" channels before exhausting more/better on the current one.

## Worked Example
A $2M/year chiropractor-niche agency claimed market saturation on a high monthly ad budgetnth of Facebook spend — while tracking no conversion data and doing zero cold outreach or content. A peer spending $30k across four platforms *in one week* made the point. The fix wasn't a new idea; it was more, measured, everywhere relevant.

## Key Takeaways
- Local Local business sequence: max warm outreach (100 WhatsApp/day) → add daily client-story posts → then cold outreach to the your local market local service business list → paid ads only once the first three print.
- When sprint inquiries stall, check the funnel constraint first (audit-request→call rate usually breaks before reach does).
- New-channel expectations: even Hormozi allows 3–6 months to crack one.
