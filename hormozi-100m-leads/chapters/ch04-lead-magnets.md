# Chapter 4 — Engage Your Leads: Offers and Lead Magnets

## Core Idea
A **lead magnet** is a complete solution to a narrow problem, free or cheap, whose solution *reveals* the next problem your core offer solves — like salty pretzels at a bar. It engages far more leads than advertising the core offer alone, for less money.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Problem-Solution Cycle**: solving one problem reveals another; pick a magnet whose revealed problem your core offer solves.
- **Three types of lead magnets**: (1) Reveal Their Problem (diagnosis), (2) Samples & Trials, (3) One Step Of A Multi-Step Process.
- **Four delivery vehicles**: software/tool, information, service, physical product → up to 12 magnets per narrow problem; rotate them.
- **7 steps**: problem+who → solution type → delivery → test name/headline (Ogilvy: headline = 80 cents of the dollar) → easy to consume (multi-format = 2–4x take rate) → darn good ("give away the secrets, sell the implementation") → CTA with reasons-to-act-now (scarcity, urgency, made-up reason — any reason beats none).
- Cost math: a free magnet can cut cost-per-customer ~3x (e.g., $3,000→a small ad budget CAC via $25 delivery cost and 10x engagement).

## Key Concepts
- Try advertising the core offer FIRST; use a magnet when prospects need proof/trust first (typical for higher-ticket).
- People who pay with their time now pay with money later.
- Paid magnets ($25 budget) can out-pull free competitors' magnets — budget buys value density.
- Grand Slam Offer principles apply to free stuff too.

## Anti-patterns
- Giving away fluff — it destroys reputation instead of building it.
- One-format magnets (PDF only); no clear next-step CTA after consumption.
- Fake scarcity instead of real capacity limits.

## Worked Example
Hormozi's failing $150/day webinar funnel (80 leads, 0 sales) was replaced overnight by a 13-minute screen-recorded case study: "FREE Case Study: How we added 213 members and $112,000 to a small gym." Calendar booked solid within a day. Same traffic, different magnet.

## Key Takeaways
- Local Agency magnets: free website-speed/booking audit (reveal), free homepage mockup (trial/sample), free GMB setup (step 1 of multi-step local-presence package). Cheap, high-perceived-value, naturally reveals "your site leaks patients → sprint fixes it."
- Always end a magnet with a specific CTA plus a reason ("I take 4 sprints a month — booking March now").
- Test headlines by polling; two words ("how to") changed book-title winners.
