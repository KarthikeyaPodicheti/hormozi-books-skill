# Chapter 5 — #1 Warm Outreach

## Core Idea
One-to-one messages to people who already know you are the cheapest, easiest, most reliable lead source — and most businesses never do it. It's how everyone should get their first five clients.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **10-step warm outreach process**: list → platform (most contacts first) → personalized greeting → 100/day × 3 attempts max → A-C-A replies → "know anybody?" offer → make it free → repeat across platforms → start charging → keep list warm.
- **A-C-A**: Acknowledge, Compliment, Ask another question steering toward your offer.
- Value-Equation-based offer script: "Do you know anyone [struggle] looking to [dream outcome] in [timeframe]? I'm taking 5 free case studies..."
- **Price ladder**: free×5 → 80% off×5 → 60% → 40%... (+20% every five until sweet spot). Referrals arriving = signal to charge.
- **9-word email** (Dean Jackson): "Are you still looking to [4-word desire]?"

## Key Concepts
- Everyone has ~1,000 contacts across phone/email/socials — your first lead list costs nothing.
- You're asking "do you know anybody," not "will you buy" — three winning outcomes (interest, referral, both) and zero pushiness.
- Free clients pay in testimonials, feedback, and referrals even when they don't convert.
- If free stuff won't move, hidden costs (time/effort/sacrifice) are too high — ask "what would make it worth it?"
- Benchmarks: ~20% engage → ~20% of those take free → ~25% of those convert ≈ **1 customer per 100 reach-outs**. Money math: 500 reach-outs/wk × 1% buying $400 offer ≈ $104k/year.

## Anti-patterns
- Generic copy-paste greetings (no personalization = ignored).
- Stopping at one attempt; skipping follow-ups.
- Working free forever because charging feels scary — let referrals be the trigger.

## Worked Example
Hormozi's first business: texted/Facebooked everyone he knew offering 12 weeks free training (charity donation + testimonial required). Six yeses → results → asked for referrals → five-six more paying clients → $4,000/monthnth replacing his salary. No website, no ads, no skills.

## Key Takeaways
- Local Local service version: WhatsApp status + personal network first — "know any local service business complaining about their website? I'm doing 5 free homepage redesigns for testimonials." Then ladder to paid sprints.
- Commit to 100 personalized reach-outs/day; expect ~1 client per 100.
- Re-probe the whole warm list quarterly with the 9-word email ("Still want more patients from your site?").
