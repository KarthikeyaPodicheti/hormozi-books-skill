# Chapter 12 — #1 Customer Referrals — Word of Mouth

## Core Idea
Referrals are the cheapest, highest-LTGP leads and the only exponential channel. They require two things most businesses skip: a product worth remarking about (goodwill) and actually asking.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Referral growth equation**: referrals in vs churn out. Referrals > churn = growth with zero advertising; equal = need ads; less = hamster wheel.
- **Goodwill = Value − Price** (never cut price; raise value).
- **Six value levers mapped to ad anatomy**: (1) Call outs→sell better-fit customers; (2) Dream outcome→set lower expectations, over-deliver (lower promises until close rate dips); (3) Likelihood→find what best customers did, force everyone to do it (Gym Launch: first ad + sale within 7 days tripled LTGP), tie guarantee to those actions; (4) Time delay→frequent small wins, updates-as-wins, force wins in first 48 hours, BAMFAM, pad timelines 50% and deliver early; (5) Effort→monthly product-improvement loop from support data; (6) CTA→keep selling them more after wins.
- **Seven ways to ask**: pay your CAC to referrer or friend (one-sided); two-sided split (Dropbox/PayPal: 39x in 15 months / $10-$10 credits → 100M users); ask at point of sale ("who else do you know who'd want this?" — salesman's half-sales-were-referrals trick); referral-as-discount-chip for a 3-way intro; referral events (bring-a-friend, spouse challenge); ongoing programs (+33% signups); unlockable bonuses for referring + testimonial.
- Gift-card tactic: expiring gift card worth ⅓ of program price makes the referrer the hero.

## Key Concepts
- "Why are my customers too embarrassed to tell everyone about my product?"
- The One Question: if advertising gods banned the core four and all customers must come from ONE customer, how would you treat them? Do that.
- Referring is social risk for the customer — goodwill lowers it, incentives raise the payoff.

## Anti-patterns
- "Everyone loves our stuff, we just need exposure" (unremarkable product).
- Waiting passively instead of asking; asking vaguely without an offer structure.
- Discounting to build goodwill — margin death, attracts worse clients.

## Worked Example
Facebook shut Gym Launch's ads off for two weeks unnoticed — revenue still ran $500k/week on word of mouth alone. Later, a room of 700 gym owners: nearly every hand went up to "heard from another gym owner." The system behind it: obsessive goodwill engineering plus $2,000 referral bounties.

## Key Takeaways
- Local Local service: after every successful project delivery, run the ask — "know another local service business whose site embarrasses them? Introduce us on WhatsApp — a credit for you both." Two-sided beats cash-only for local professional networks.
- Engineer fast wins: send progress screenshots daily during the 7-day sprint (updates are wins).
- Target: ≥25% of new sprints from referrals (Roadmap L4 threshold).
