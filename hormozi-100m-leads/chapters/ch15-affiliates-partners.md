# Chapter 15 — #4 Affiliates and Partners

## Core Idea
Affiliates are businesses whose warm audiences contain your buyers, paid to promote you. Highest-leverage lead getter: each one added compounds monthly. ALAN scaled to $1.7M/monthnth in six months on a few super-affiliates.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **6-step affiliate army**: (1) Find ideal affiliates ("Who's got my leads?" — what they buy/where they go/services they use; categories: software, products, equipment, services, groups, events), (2) Make them an offer (Value Equation pitched as "make more money from your current customers"), (3) Qualify via investment — make them a customer and/or certified expert (charge 10–20% of average affiliate first-year earnings for onboarding; raise minimum every 5 signups), (4) Pay them — pay for new + repeat customers at max allowable CAC, split into tiers: 25% CAC on signup, 50% on activation, 100% on sustained performance → blended payout < max CAC improves LTGP:CAC from 3:1 toward 4:1+, (5) Activate with launches — **whisper-tease-shout** (curiosity drip → reveal value elements 2×/week → all-out CTAs + bonuses final 3 days), (6) Keep them advertising via integration.
- **Integration ladder** (easiest→hardest): affiliates GIVE your lead magnet away with their sales (your magnet strengthens their offer) → affiliates SELL your lead magnet and keep 100% (you upsell the core) → affiliates SELL your core offer for a lifetime revenue split, never capped.
- **Affiliate economics**: cost-to-get-affiliate vs gross profit of customers they bring (example: $4k CAC vs $54k leftover profit = 12.5:1).

## Key Concepts
- Case studies: free-LLC lead magnet distributed by entrepreneur-training partners ($50M business, zero ad spend); Prestige Labs gyms selling supplements at nutrition orientations; chiropractors running posture workshops inside gyms then integrating free adjustments into memberships.
- Treat affiliates like customers — their goodwill determines your lead flow.

## Anti-patterns
- Recruiting affiliates who won't invest (no skin = no promotion).
- Flat payouts that reward dead accounts; capping commissions (kills long-term motivation).
- Building the program before having 10–20 pilot affiliates' feedback.

## Worked Example
Prestige Labs launch week one: $150k against $3M inventory — disaster. Week four: $450k+ as trained gym-owner affiliates activated. No ads, no sales team; 400+ affiliates averaging 300 orders/day thereafter. The machine printed because affiliates won first.

## Key Takeaways
- Local Local affiliate map (your local market): local service suppliers & lab techs, local service equipment dealers, local service business interior contractors, interior-design photographers & material vendors, CA/loan agents serving local service businesses, local service-college alumni groups. Offer: referral commission per sprint or reciprocal client introductions.
- Start integration-light: give partners a co-branded "free website audit" voucher to hand clients — costs nothing, warms every introduction.
- Pay performance-tiered commissions only after delivery money lands.
