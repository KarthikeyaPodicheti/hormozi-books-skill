# Chapter 3 — Leads Alone Aren't Enough

## Core Idea
A **lead** is simply a person you can contact. What advertising must produce is an **engaged lead**: someone who *shows interest* in what you sell. Engaged leads are the true output of advertising.

## Frameworks Introduced
- Lead vs Engaged Lead distinction.
- Implied question the rest of the book answers: how do we get leads to engage? (Answer: offers and lead magnets.)

## Key Concepts
- Words shape behavior: teams that call scraped contacts "leads" optimize the wrong things.
- Contact info given willingly (form fill, follow, reply) = engaged; bought lists = merely contactable.
- The raised hand is where lead generation ends and sales begins (deliberate scope boundary).

## Anti-patterns
- Counting impressions, followers, or list size as results.
- Buying "lead lists" and expecting them to behave like inbound interest.

## Worked Example
Hormozi spent six months unable to define "lead" — realizing the confusion itself was the problem forced him to rebuild his entire advertising practice around one measurable output: people who show interest.

## Key Takeaways
- Track engaged leads (replies, bookings, DMs) as your single north-star metric.
- For Local Agency: WhatsApp replies and audit requests are the metric; scraped local service business numbers are just raw material.

## Connects To
