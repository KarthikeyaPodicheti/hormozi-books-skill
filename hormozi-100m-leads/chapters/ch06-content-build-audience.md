# Chapter 6 — #2 Post Free Content Part I: Build An Audience

## Core Idea
Content converts 1-to-many leverage: say it once, reach thousands. The content isn't the asset — the audience is. Volume wins: 10x content = 10x audience growth speed.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **The Content Unit — Hook / Retain / Reward**: hook attention (topic × headline × format), retain attention (curiosity), reward attention (deliver promised value per second).
- **Topic sources**: Far Past (life lessons), Recent Past (meetings, client stories — gold mine), Present (capture ideas instantly), Trending, Manufactured.
- **Headline components** (use 2+): recency, relevancy, celebrity, proximity, conflict, unusual, ongoing.
- **Retention devices**: lists, steps, stories — all embed unresolved questions.
- Long form = linked content units; start short, build length as skill grows.

## Key Concepts
- Format-match the platform: look like what your audience already enjoys.
- Trade-offs vs outreach: less personalization, crowded competition, copycats require constant innovation.
- Personal experience is unarguable and uncopyable — "there's only one of you."

## Anti-patterns
- Posting generic advice anyone could post (puddle problem: a small local player loses to bigger track records).
- Blaming "short attention spans" — audiences have high standards, not short spans; there's no too long, only too boring.
- Measuring nothing: track total followers/reach AND monthly growth rate.

## Worked Example
Influencer coaching (a premium coaching package) reduced to one instruction: "You posted once today; I posted three times. Do more." Six months of 10x output → significant audience growth. Later analysis: 78% of buyers had consumed ≥2 pieces of long-form content before booking a call.

## Key Takeaways
- Puddles→ponds→lakes→oceans: for Local Agency, dominate "your local market local service business marketing" content before attempting general agency content.
- Endless topic supply = client stories: every sprint is 2-3 posts (before/after screenshots, "why Dr. X's site wasn't getting bookings").
- Proximity headlines crush locally: "This Banjara Hills local service business doubled bookings with one page change."
