# Chapter 13 — #2 Employees

## Core Idea
Lead-getting employees convert your hours into theirs: trade 40 hours of doing for 4 hours of managing, repeatedly. This is also what turns a founder-dependent job into a sellable asset ($2M profit ≈ $10M+ valuation only if it runs without you).

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Internal Core Four mapping**: warm outreach→network asks; cold outreach→recruiting outreach; content→job postings; paid ads→promoted postings; referrals→employee referrals; agencies→staffing firms; affiliates→associations/guilds/listservs.
- **3D training model — Document, Demonstrate, Duplicate**: write a checklist so exact a stranger could follow it; demo it; watch them run it; fix the checklist until duplication succeeds.
- **Payroll efficiency metric**: Total payroll ÷ total engaged leads = cost per engaged lead (Hormozi: $100k/month content team ÷ 30k leads = $3.33/lead).
- Diagnosis question for underperformance: "Do my engaged leads have the problem I solve and money to spend?" No → advertising problem; yes but not buying → sales problem.

## Key Concepts
- Replace yourself ASAP; "if you want it done right" is a training failure, not a talent truth.
- Hire for work ethic + coachability at ground level; group interviews beat 1-on-1 screening bottlenecks (40 interviews → hire became weeks not quarters).
- Training rules: praise following directions even with wrong results (then fix checklist), feedback one step at a time, no punishment during learning, retrain on any major performance dip.

## Anti-patterns
- Competing with your own employees instead of enabling them.
- Hiring "experienced geniuses" for simple lead-getting roles.
- Blaming reps when the funnel has an advertising problem.

## Worked Example
Cold-outreach sales missed goals two straight quarters. Root cause chain: rep churn every 4 weeks × 1-in-4 offer acceptance × 1 qualified candidate per 10 screenings = ~1 hire/week. Switching to group interviews fixed hiring within six weeks; quarterly cold-outreach sales doubled.

## Key Takeaways
- Local Agency: before scaling headcount, document your own audit→WhatsApp→close process as a checklist; the first hire duplicates it.
- Even a part-time intern running 100 cold DMs/day at documented quality transforms capacity.
- Judge hires on direction-following speed, not brilliance.
