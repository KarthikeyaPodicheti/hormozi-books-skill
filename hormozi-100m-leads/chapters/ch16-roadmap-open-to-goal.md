# Chapter 16 — Advertising in Real Life: Open To Goal + The Roadmap

## Core Idea
Volume is the difference between "advertising doesn't work" and advertising working. Upgrade Rule of 100 to **open-to-goal**: work until a fixed number of outcomes happens, whatever hours it takes. Then climb the seven-level roadmap deliberately.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Minimum viable test sizes**: 300 flyers ≠ a test; test at scale where 0.5% response is measurable (mentor's rule: 5,000/day). Most people do 1/40th to 1/1500th of required volume and blame the channel.
- **Open-to-goal**: commit to outcomes (5 signups/day), not hours; pair with the habit stack — wake early (4–5am), start immediately, no meetings till noon.
- **One-page advertising checklist (5 steps)**: pick lead type (customers/affiliates/employees/agencies) → pick Rule of 100 or open-to-goal → fill that channel's checklist → do it daily until you can afford to hire it out → switch target to employees and repeat.
- **Seven levels**: L1 friends know (warm outreach) → L2 consistent inputs (warm + content maximized) → L3 hired help → L4 referrals ≥25% of new customers (product obsession checkpoint) → L5 two methods × multiple platforms (+lead getters) → L6 executive-run departments → L7 TBD at $1B.
- **The Many-Sided Die fable**: you can't see your die's sides; rolling red turns sides green; the only guarantees are (1) more rolls = better, (2) quitting loses.

## Key Concepts
- Expect 3–6 months minimum to crack any NEW lead source even when experienced; switching methods after early losses guarantees failure.
- Set aside 1–10% of ad budget for un-returnable experiments ("permission to fail" → tripled budget → $400k→$1.8M/monthnth within months).
- Inputs are controllable; outputs follow. "You either win or you learn."

## Anti-patterns
- Judging channels on tiny samples (the 300-flyer lesson).
- Writing 100-page plans instead of a one-page checklist you either did or didn't do.
- Letting product quality slip while scaling ads (where most businesses die at L4).

## Worked Example
Gym-launch-era compounding: testing budget unlocked new audiences ($1.2M/month) → discovered text-blast follow-up ($1.8M) → ads production push ($2.5M) → affiliate program stack (+$1.5M) → $4M+/monthnth; portfolio later $16M/monthnth. Each leap was a test doubled-down-on, not a brainstorm.

## Key Takeaways
- Local Local business daily plan: one page — target (local service business sprint clients), channel (e.g., cold WhatsApp), daily quota (100 messages / 60 audit-minutes), kill-or-scale rules. Do it before noon.
- Don't evaluate any channel before ~2 weeks × 100 actions/day.
- Track which level you're on; fix referrals before buying growth.
