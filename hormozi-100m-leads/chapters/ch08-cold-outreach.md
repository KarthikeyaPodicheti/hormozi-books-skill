# Chapter 8 — #3 Cold Outreach

## Core Idea
Cold outreach = private 1-to-1 messages to strangers. It's a numbers game that solves three problems in order: no way to contact them → they ignore you → they're not interested. Slow to ramp (expect months), but reliable, invisible to competitors, and platform-proof.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **List building ladder**: scraping software → list brokers → elbow grease (manual assembly = freshest lists).
- **Personalize** (act like you know them): 1–3 researched facts so a cold message feels warm.
- **Big Fast Value**: give away something they'd actually pay for, within seconds. Upgrading from a weak magnet ("free game-planning call") to real free work 3x'd take rates.
- **Volume engine**: automate delivery + distribution where ethical; follow up multiple times multiple ways; re-hit the list every 3–6 months (circumstances change).

## Key Concepts
- Reverse-engineer outcomes: X contacts → Y customers, so solve for X ("100 emails per customer → need 3 people sending 111/day for 100/monthnth").
- Benchmarks: cold calls ≈ 4 engaged leads/100 dials; personalized email/DM ≈ 3% reply (portfolio example 4%); expect 2–3 conversations before a higher-ticket close.
- Health check: cost per customer < 1/3 LTGP (3:1 floor); great operators hit 30:1.
- Seven benefits: one message to perfect, secrecy, reliability, few platform changes, light compliance, sellable business (no founder dependency), hard to copy.
- Email first, then call "to follow up my email" — every non-response is ammunition for the next touch.

## Anti-patterns
- Tinkering with scripts before 100 conversations / 10,000 sends.
- Weak lead magnets dressed as offers ("quick question!" with nothing behind it).
- Giving up at week 8 — Hormozi's team demanded shutdown twice before month 6 inflected.

## Worked Example
COVID killed Hormozi's ad-dependent businesses. A rejected salesman candidate who'd run a $10M/monthnth pure-cold-outreach team rebuilt cold outreach internally: Sept 0 sales → Oct $32k → Dec $64k → monthly doubling → millions/monthnth. The unlock was swapping the lead magnet for genuinely free service.

## Key Takeaways
- Local Local service playbook: build a Google Maps/Instagram list of your local market local service businesses & local service businesses; open each WhatsApp with a personalized observation (their reviews, their site speed); deliver a real mini-audit with screenshots unprompted; follow up on WhatsApp + call across 2 weeks; recycle the list quarterly.
- Personalize heavily — the local market is small enough (thousands, not millions) that every message should feel 1-to-1.
- Expect ramp time: budget 8–12 weeks before judging the channel.
