# Chapter 7 — #2 Post Free Content Part II: Monetize Your Audience

## Core Idea
Monetize with the give:ask ratio — deposit goodwill with valuable content before withdrawing with offers. Best version: **give until they ask** (give publicly, let buyers self-select privately).

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Give : Ask ratio**: TV survives ~3.5:1; growing platforms over-give massively. Under-ask to keep growing.
- **Two ask structures**: Integrated asks (inside long-form content, after value moments; e.g., 3×30s pitches in an hour podcast) and Intermittent asks (10 pure-give posts, then 1 promo post — better for short-form).
- **Ask content options**: pitch the lead magnet (lower risk) or the core offer directly (Value-Equation script: "Looking for 5 [avatar]... guarantee...").
- **Scaling strategies**: Depth-then-width (max one platform then add) vs Width-then-depth (all platforms early, maximize together; repurposing efficiency).

## Key Concepts
- Asking slows growth by design — the moment you start selling is the moment you trade reach for revenue.
- Content creates sales tools: keep a "greatest hits" list labeled by objection solved; send in DMs/calls.
- Free content retains paying customers too — customers count your free content into their ROI.
- "How I" beats "how to": share experience, never preach. Repeat yourself — 1 in 5 followers doesn't know your offer even when you post it daily.

## Anti-patterns
- Over-asking (a friend's podcast shrank from pitching too often inside episodes).
- Pre-scheduling everything — manual posting's tight feedback loop improves quality.
- Making broad-market content as a small local business.

## Worked Example
Gym Launch's paid ads "stopped working"; the team blamed creative/copy. Leila asked: what did we stop? Alex had switched from gym-specific content to generic business content. The survey showed 78% of buyers consumed 2+ pieces of gym content pre-call — free content had been silently doing the nurturing.

## Key Takeaways
- Local Agency cadence: ~4-5 give posts per promo post on Instagram; promos pitch the free audit or a sprint slot ("2 sprint slots left this month").
- Repurpose one client-story reel across Reels/LinkedIn/WhatsApp status.
- Track monthly follower + reach growth rate, not vanity totals.
