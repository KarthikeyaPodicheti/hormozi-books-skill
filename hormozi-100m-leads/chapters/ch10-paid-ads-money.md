# Chapter 10 — #4 Run Paid Ads Part II: Money Stuff

## Core Idea
Ads are an ROI machine, not an art project: "good enough is good enough," then scale. Master two numbers — **LTGP:CAC ≥ 3:1** and **30-day payback (client financed acquisition)** — and ads become a money printer.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **LTGP (lifetime gross profit)**: total customer revenue minus delivery costs, lifetime. Not revenue-LTV.
- **LTGP : CAC ratio**: profitability test; 3:1 is the observed floor below which businesses stall. Levers: cheaper CAC (better advertising) or higher LTGP (better business model).
- Diagnostic: CAC < 3x industry average → fix the model; CAC > 3x average → fix the ads.
- **Three phases of scaling**: Track Money (set up attribution before spending) → Lose Money (expected tuition; kill fast, scale winners hard) → Print Money (reverse-budget from customer goals, +20% padding).
- **Test-budget rule**: allow 2x thirty-day cash collected per customer before killing a new ad; kill at 1x if zero leads.
- **Client Financed Acquisition / payback period**: engineer upsells so gross profit collected within 30 days ≥ CAC → credit-card float funds infinite scaling. Example: $15/month membership, $5 cost, $30 CAC, 10-month life = 3.3:1 but 3-month payback; a $100 upsell taken by 1-in-5 adds $20 avg → break-even in 30 days.

## Key Concepts
- Winners and losers in an industry have similar CACs — the difference is how much they make per customer.
- Don't confuse sales problems with advertising problems: qualified leads not buying = sales problem (one company quit six inches from gold, ~$30M mistake).
- Best free content usually makes the best ads; UGC testimonials are a free ad pipeline.
- Scaling math: want N customers × CAC × 1.2 = monthly budget.

## Anti-patterns
- Spending without tracking; judging ads weekly instead of over months.
- Starving winning ads and zombie-ing losers.
- Optimizing CAC forever while the model earns pennies — "costs approach zero, earnings go to infinity."

## Worked Example
$15 membership example above: same numbers, one added upsell converts a cash-flow-crippled campaign into self-funding growth — the exact mechanism behind seven straight years scaling companies past $1M/monthnth without outside capital.

## Key Takeaways
- Local Business math sketch: your base price sprint with a maintenance/retainer upsell must be engineered so first-30-day gross profit covers acquisition cost before chasing scale with ads; until then warm outreach and referrals fund growth.
- Compute LTGP including retainers and repeat sprints (site refreshes, GMB management) — a local service business is worth far more than one sprint.
- If engaged local service businesses aren't buying, fix the sprint offer/sales call before blaming lead sources.
