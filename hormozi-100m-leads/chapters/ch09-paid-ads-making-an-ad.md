# Chapter 9 — #4 Run Paid Ads Part I: Making An Ad

## Core Idea
Paid ads = renting eyeballs: guaranteed reach, unguaranteed return. The game is efficiency, not creativity — narrow who sees the ad until the right people respond profitably, then expand puddle→pond→lake→ocean.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **4-step ad process**: pick platform (you use it, can target it, know formats, can afford minimums) → target (lookalike audiences from customer/warm/cold lists + filters) → craft ad (**Call Out + Value + CTA**) → capture contact info (landing page matching the ad, click-to-close continuity).
- **Call Out types**: labels ("your local market local service businesses"), yes-questions, if-then statements, ridiculous results; nonverbal: contrast, likeness (dress/quack like your audience; mascots), the scene (visual yes-questions). Local-area + type-of-person callouts are timeless.
- **What-Who-When framework**: each Value Equation driver AND its opposite (dream outcome/nightmare, likelihood/risk, delay/speed, effort/ease) × perspectives who grant status (spouse, peers, rivals) × past/present/future timelines = unlimited angles. First five seconds matter most (20x improvement focusing there).
- **CTA discipline**: spell out exactly what to do; short memorable URLs; layer scarcity/urgency/reasons.

## Key Concepts
- Ads are riskier but fastest-scaling channel — learn it last, funded by the other three.
- Specific targeting wins early money that later funds broader audiences; efficiency falls as you scale but absolute profit rises.
- Consistency principle (Cialdini): frame next steps as continuation of actions just taken.

## Anti-patterns
- Frankenstein funnels (ad promise ≠ landing page).
- Testing everything except the first five seconds.
- Vague CTAs or clever-but-unclear instructions.

## Worked Example
First-ever ad: an all-caps text-only Facebook post — "LOOKING FOR 5 CHINO HILLS RESIDENTS FOR A FREE 6-WEEK CHALLENGE; you let us use before/afters." a small ad budget → 19 signups at a low front-end price → significant front-end revenue. That ugly offer became the biggest promotion in gym industry history (industry-scale revenue driven). Callout + genuine value + clear CTA beat production polish.

## Key Takeaways
- Local Local service ad starter: callout "Attention your local market local service businesses…", value = "we'll show you why patients leave your website (free 10-point audit)", CTA = WhatsApp us 'AUDIT'. Landing page = simple form or WhatsApp deep link.
- Seed lookalikes/custom audiences from past clients' contact lists first.
- Steal structure: study ads targeting your niches instead of skipping them.
