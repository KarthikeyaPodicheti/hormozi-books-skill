# Chapter 2 — The Problem This Book Solves

## Core Idea
"You're not getting as many leads as you want because you're not advertising enough. Period." Businesses grow only two ways: more customers or more value per customer. This book is the customer side.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **The four lead objectives**: get More leads, Better leads, Cheaper leads, Reliably (from many places).
- Doubling leads doubles the business, all else equal.

## Key Concepts
- Advertising = "the process of making known." Ignorance of your existence is the bottleneck, not quality.
- The book progresses from zero skills/monthney (Section II) to maximum skill/monthney (Section IV) — channel difficulty is sequenced.
- Masters never stop doing basics; plateaus usually mean abandoned fundamentals, not exhausted markets.

## Anti-patterns
- Waiting for referrals while doing no deliberate advertising.
- Treating advertising as a one-time project rather than daily inputs.

## Worked Example
Hormozi frames his whole portfolio playbook around this: every portfolio company gets diagnosed on the four objectives — if leads are expensive, cheapen them; if unreliable, add channels; if few, raise volume.

## Key Takeaways
- Diagnose any growth problem as more/better/cheaper/reliable leads before touching anything else.
- Volume of advertising inputs is the variable almost everyone leaves on the table.
