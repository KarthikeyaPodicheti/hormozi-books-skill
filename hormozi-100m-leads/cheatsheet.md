# Cheatsheet — $100M Leads for Local Agency

One-screen reference. Details in SKILL.md; rules & thresholds in patterns.md.

## The Core Four at a Glance

| Channel | Who | How | Daily quota (Rule of 100) | Expect |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Warm outreach | People who know you | Personal WhatsApp/text/call → "know anybody?" offer | 100/day, ≤3 touches each | ~1 client/100 |
| Content | Warm audience (public) | Hook-Retain-Reward client stories; give:ask ≥4:1 | 100 min/day, ≥1 post | Compounds slowly |
| Cold outreach | Strangers (private) | List → personalize → Big Fast Value audit → follow up | 100/day + recycle qtrly | ≥3% reply; 8-12 wk ramp |
| Paid ads | Strangers (public) | Call Out + Value + CTA; hyper-local targeting | 100 min/day | Only after payback works |

Amplifiers: Referrals > Partners/affiliates > Employees > Agencies. Get them USING the core four.

## Lead Magnet Menu (cheap, local-business)
- **Reveal**: free 10-point website/GMB audit w/ screenshots (default weapon)
- **Trial/sample**: free homepage mockup or speed fix
- **Step 1 of multi-step**: free GMB setup → upsell full local-presence package
CTA every magnet: "I take 4 sprints/monthnth — [specific CTA] because [real capacity limit]."

## WhatsApp Cold Script Skeleton
1. Personalized opener (their reviews/site/IG) — 1-2 lines
2. One specific observed problem ("site takes 9s to load on mobile")
3. Big Fast Value: "I recorded a 3-min audit showing what's costing you bookings — want it?"
4. Silence → follow-up ×2 over 10 days → re-hit list in 90 days
5. On reply: A-C-A (Acknowledge, Compliment, Ask toward their patients/bookings)

## Referral Ask (after every delivery win)
"Thrilled the new site is booking patients! Know one other local service business whose site embarrasses them? Introduce us on WhatsApp and you both get [credit/gift]." Two-sided. Live intro. At point of delight.

## Money Rules
- LTGP:CAC ≥ 3:1 or don't scale ads. Count retainers in LTGP.
- Ad test budget: kill at 1× 30-day cash if zero leads; 2× if leads but no sales; 10x winners.
- Payback: engineer first-30-day gross profit ≥ CAC before ad scaling.
- Reverse-budget: desired clients × CAC × 1.2 = monthly ad budget.

## Cadence
- Rule of 100 daily per active channel; judge channels only after 2 weeks × 100/day.
- 1 funnel test/week at the biggest drop-off step.
- Stalled? More → Better → New (placement → platform → channel).
- Referrals <25% of new business? Fix goodwill before buying growth.

## Escalation Ladder (Local Agency)
Free sprints (proof) → warm outreach ladder (paid) → content puddle (your local market local service businesses/designers) → cold outreach list → partner vouchers (suppliers, photographers, CAs) → paid ads → hire → affiliates at scale.
