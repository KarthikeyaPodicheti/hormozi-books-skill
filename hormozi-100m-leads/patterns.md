# Patterns — Decision Rules & Thresholds ($100M Leads × Local Agency)

Decision trees and numeric thresholds from the book, weighted for Local Agency (website sprints; local service businesses & local service businesses; your local market).

## Channel Selection Tree

```
Need leads now?
├─ Zero clients / no proof yet?
│ └─ WARM OUTREACH ONLY. 100 WhatsApp/texts-day × 3 days per contact.
│ First 5 sprints FREE (testimonial + feedback + 3 intros required).
│ Raise price every 5 closes: free → 80% off → 60% → 40%...
├─ Have 3+ happy clients but leads stalled?
│ ├─ Time > money? → CONTENT (daily client-story posts) + keep warm outreach.
│ └─ Money > time? → COLD OUTREACH (local service business/designer list) before paid ads.
├─ Warm list exhausted + content running?
│ └─ COLD OUTREACH: Google Maps/IG scrape → personalized WhatsApp w/ free audit.
└─ Cold outreach converts ≥ benchmark AND 30-day payback works?
 └─ PAID ADS (start a test ad budget/day tests; see budget rules below).
Amplifiers (any time, cheapest-first): ask every delivered client for 2 intros →
recruit 5-20 local partners (suppliers, photographers, CAs) → hire help last.
```

## Numeric Thresholds

| Metric | Threshold | Action if below |
|---|---|---|
| Warm outreach engagement | ~20% reply | Rewrite opener; add personalization |
| Free-offer take rate | ~1 in 5 repliers | Magnet too weak — add real deliverable |
| Free→paid conversion | ~25% | Fix offer/sales call, not the channel |
| Overall warm yield | ~1 customer/100 reach-outs | Volume problem — check daily count first |
| Cold DM/call engagement | ≥3% reply | Personalization or Big-Fast-Value too weak |
| Cold ramp patience | 8–12 weeks min | Do NOT kill before ~1,400 touches |
| New-channel judgment | 3–6 months | Expected even by Hormozi |
| Give:ask ratio (content) | ≥4:1; ideally give-until-they-ask | Growth stalls/shrinks below 3.5:1 |
| Content asks | 1 promo per ~5 posts | Cut promos |
| LTGP:CAC | **≥3:1 floor** | Below → fix model (retainers/upsells), not ads |
| CAC vs industry | <3x industry avg = fine | Above → fix advertising |
| Ad-test kill limit | 0 leads at 1× 30-day cash; else 2× | Kill fast; 10x the winner |
| Referral share of new clients | ≥25% target | Product goodwill problem first |
| Test cadence | 1 test/wk/platform at biggest drop-off | Abandon after 4 failed tries |

## Decision Rules

1. **Volume before verdict**: any "channel doesn't work" claim requires ~100 actions/day for 2+ weeks first. Sub-threshold tests prove nothing (300-flyer rule).
2. **Constraint rule**: improve the funnel step with the largest % drop-off (for Local Agency usually audit-request → call/show-up, not reach).
3. **Payback rule**: don't scale paid ads until first-30-day gross profit per client ≥ CAC. With your base price sprints this means selling a maintenance retainer/GMB upsell at delivery — otherwise ads stay funded by savings.
4. **Referral-before-scale rule** (Roadmap L4): get referrals to 25% of new sprints BEFORE pouring money into ads; otherwise you're renting growth atop a leaky product.
5. **More→Better→New order**: double inputs first; then weekly constraint tests; only then new placement → new platform → new core-four activity.
6. **Personalization ∝ scarcity rule**: small local market (~thousands of local service businesses/designers) → personalize every message; never blast templates.
7. **Big-Fast-Value rule for cold**: send the actual mini-audit unprompted (screenshots, load time, missing booking path). Weak magnets ("quick question?") waste the list — lists are finite locally.
8. **Two-sided referral rule**: always structure referral asks as both-sides-win (credit to referrer + discount/gift to friend) and demand a live WhatsApp group-intro, not just a name.
9. **Partner-integration rule**: give partners (local service suppliers, photographers) a co-branded free-audit voucher to distribute — costs nothing, pre-warms every intro; pay commission only after client payment clears.
10. **Skill-purchase rule**: any freelancer/agency hired must include knowledge transfer + downgrade-to-consulting clause; cancel when in-house beats them twice.
11. **Free-first-five rule** for any NEW service line (landing pages for local service businesses, GMB management): five free case studies restart the flywheel cheaply.
