# Hormozi $100M Leads — Applied to Local Agencies

Source: Alex Hormozi, *$100M Leads* (Acquisition.com Vol. II). Use when choosing or scaling a lead-generation channel for Local Agency, writing WhatsApp/cold DM scripts to local service businesses or local service businesses, building lead magnets, asking for referrals, deciding ad budgets, or diagnosing "not enough leads."

**The core diagnosis**: if you're not getting as many leads as you want, you're not advertising enough. There are only four ways one person can let people know about your stuff. Pick one, max it out with volume.

## Core Frameworks

**Lead vs Engaged Lead**: a **lead** is simply a person you can contact. An **engaged lead** is someone who *shows interest* in what you sell (replies, books, DMs, gives contact info). Engaged leads are the true output of advertising — count replies and bookings, not impressions or scraped contacts. For Local Agency: a local service business's number in a Google Maps scrape is a lead; a reply of "send details" on WhatsApp is an engaged lead.

**Lead Magnets**: a complete solution to a narrow problem — free or cheap — that reveals the next problem your core offer solves (salty pretzels make you thirsty). A good lead magnet engages ideal buyers, out-pulls the core offer alone, gets consumed, and pre-sells the core offer. Three types: **(1) Reveal Their Problem** (diagnosis — e.g., a free website audit showing a local business site loads in 8s and leaks booking patients), **(2) Samples & Trials** (brief full access — e.g., free homepage redesign mockup), **(3) One Step Of A Multi-Step Process** (e.g., free "Google My Business setup" as step 1 of a full local-presence package). Four delivery vehicles: software/tool, information, service (do work free), physical product. Build via 7 steps: pick narrow problem → pick solution type → pick delivery vehicle → test name/headline → make easy to consume → make it genuinely good ("give away the secrets, sell the implementation") → clear CTA with reasons to act now (scarcity, urgency, any reason beats no reason). Math: a lead magnet can cut cost-per-customer ~3x by tripling engagement before the sales call. For a your base price sprint business, the free website/Google-listing audit is the highest-leverage asset you own.

**Four Lead Channels / The Core Four**: every advertisement is a combination of audience (warm = gave permission / cold = strangers) × communication mode (1-to-1 private / 1-to-many public):
- **Warm Outreach** = 1-to-1 to warm audience (calls, texts, WhatsApp to people who know you)
- **Post Free Content** = 1-to-many to warm audience (Instagram posts, reels)
- **Cold Outreach** = 1-to-1 to cold audience (WhatsApp/DM/calls to scraped local service business lists)
- **Paid Ads** = 1-to-many to cold audience (Meta ads targeted at your local market local service businesses)
Plus amplifiers (**the Four Lead Getters**, Section IV): customers→referrals, employees, agencies, affiliates/partners. These aren't things you *do*; they're people you acquire *using* the core four, who then advertise for you. Order of attack when starting: warm outreach → content → cold outreach → paid ads (skills and cash from earlier channels fund later ones).

**Warm Outreach protocol**: everyone has a list (phone, WhatsApp, email, socials ≈ your first 1,000 leads). 10 steps: get your list → pick platform with most contacts → personalize the greeting → reach out to **100 people per day, up to 3 days in a row** until response → use **A-C-A** on replies (Acknowledge, Compliment, Ask another question steering toward your offer) → after 3-4 exchanges make the "know anybody" offer built from the Value Equation ("I'm taking 5 free case studies...") → start FREE (first five clients free for testimonial+feedback+referrals) → repeat across platforms → **start charging once referrals arrive** (raise price every 5 clients: free→80% off→60% off...) → keep the list warm and probe with Dean Jackson's **9-word email**: "Are you still looking to [4-word desire]?" Benchmarks: ~20% of warm reach-outs engage; ~1 in 5 repliers takes a free offer; ~1 in 4 free converts to paid ≈ **1 customer per 100 reach-outs**.

**Hook–Retain–Reward (the content unit)**: all audience-growing content does three things. **Hook**: topic × headline × platform-native format — headline components: recency, relevancy, celebrity, proximity (local!), conflict, unusual, ongoing; use 2+. **Retain**: embed unresolved questions via lists, steps, stories. **Reward**: satisfy the promise at high value-per-second; there's no such thing as too long, only too boring. Long content = linked content units. Topic sources: far past, recent past (client stories/testimonials), present notes, trending, manufactured. Scale via depth-then-width or width-then-depth. Monetize with the **give:ask ratio** — TV sustains only ~3.5:1; growing platforms give vastly more. Hormozi's rule: **give until they ask** (give publicly, ask privately). If you must ask: integrated asks (after value moments) on long-form, intermittent asks (10 gives : 1 ask) on short-form; pitch the lead magnet if unsure. Key lesson: 78% of clients had consumed ≥2 long-form pieces before booking — content silently warms every other channel. For Local Agency: puddles-first content ("website mistakes I fixed for a your local market local service business this week"), not generic marketing content.

**Cold Outreach protocol**: strangers create three problems — no way to contact them, they ignore you, they're not interested. Solutions: **(1) Build a list** — scraping software → list brokers → elbow grease (manually assembled lists are freshest; for us: Google Maps + Instagram for your local market local service businesses/local service businesses). **(2) Personalize + Big Fast Value** — 1-3 researched personal facts so the cold message feels warm (their local service business's reviews, their Instagram), then give something so valuable they'd normally pay for it (a real audit with screenshots, not "can I pick your brain"). Swapping a weak lead magnet for genuine free work 3x'd Hormozi's take rates. **(3) Volume + follow-up** — automate delivery/distribution where ethical, follow up multiple times multiple ways, then re-hit the same list every 3-6 months because circumstances change. Benchmarks: cold calls ~4 engaged leads per 100 dials; personalized emails/DMs ~3% reply rate (aim 3%+); expect 2-3 conversations before a sale; healthy when cost-per-customer < 1/3 of lifetime gross profit. Cold outreach is reliable, private (competitors can't see it), platform-change-proof, and hard to copy — but slow to ramp (expect months, not weeks).

**Ad basics (Call Out + Value + CTA)**: pick a platform you use and can target; target via lookalike audiences of existing customers + interest/geography filters (start hyper-local and specific — efficiency first, expand later); craft ads with **Call Out** (labels like "your local market local service businesses", yes-questions, if-then statements, ridiculous results; local + type-of-person callouts are evergreen), **Value** via the What-Who-When framework (each Value Equation driver + its opposite, shown through spouse/peers/rivals perspectives across past-present-future), and a spelled-out **CTA**. Get permission to contact: simple landing page matching the ad, click-to-close continuity.

**LTGP : CAC (lifetime gross profit vs cost to acquire)**: measure **lifetime GROSS PROFIT** (all revenue − all delivery costs over the customer's life), not revenue-LTV. Profitable ads = LTGP > CAC; **3:1 is the floor** — nearly every struggling business Hormozi invested in was below 3:1, and lifting it above 3 flipped them. Levers: lower CAC (better ads/outreach) or raise LTGP (better business model — upsells, retainers). Diagnostic: if your CAC < 3x industry average, fix the model (LTGP), not the ads. Payback: budget new-ad tests at **2x thirty-day cash collected per customer** before killing an ad (kill at 1x if zero leads). **Client Financed Acquisition / payback period**: engineer offers so gross profit collected in the first 30 days ≥ CAC — then ads fund themselves via credit-card float and scale is unlimited. Scaling phases: Track Money → Lose Money (expected; kill losers fast, 10x winners) → Print Money (reverse-budget from "how many customers do I want," pad +20%).

**More Better New**: when leads stall, only three moves exist. **More**: Rule of 100 — 100 primary advertising actions per day for 100 straight days. **Better**: test one thing per week per platform at the biggest drop-off point (the **constraint**); log results; move on after 4 failed attempts. **New** (only after more+better exhausted): new placement → new platform → new core-four activity. Beware the Size-of-the-Pie Fallacy: nobody saturates a market at a high monthly ad budgetnth spend.

**Referrals (word of mouth)**: highest-quality, cheapest, exponential lead source. Growth equation: referrals in vs churn out — grow when referrals > churn. Most businesses fail at both prerequisites: product isn't remarkable, and they never ask. Build goodwill = value minus price (never discount — add value): six levers mapped to the ad structure — sell better-fit customers (call outs), under-promise/over-deliver (dream outcome), find what top customers did and force everyone to do it (perceived likelihood), faster/monthre frequent wins + updates in the first 48 hours, BAMFAM (book-a-meeting-from-a-meeting), deliver early with padded timelines (time delay), keep improving the product (effort), keep selling them more (CTA). Then **ask**: treat referrals like an offer — one-sided (pay your CAC to referrer or friend), two-sided (Dropbox/PayPal style split), ask at point of purchase ("who else do you know who'd want this?"), referral-as-negotiation-chip (discount for a 3-way intro), bring-a-friend events, ongoing programs, unlockable bonuses. Ask for a 3-way introduction, right when they buy.

**Employees / Agencies / Affiliates (other lead getters)**: Employees — hire via the internal core four (job posts = content, recruiting = cold outreach), train with **Document-Demonstrate-Duplicate** checklists; trade doing-hours for managing-hours repeatedly. Agencies — don't rent results forever; hire with an explicit learning contract ("6 months, teach my team, downgrade to consulting once we beat your numbers"); good-agency checklist: word-of-mouth reputation, waiting list, realistic expectations, no hacks, expensive. Affiliates — businesses whose audiences contain your buyers (for us: local service suppliers, interior-design photographers, local service business fit-out contractors, CA/loan agents serving local service businesses); offer them money for promoting you; qualify by making them invest (customer/expert); pay up to max allowable CAC in tiers (25%/50%/100% by activation level); activate with whisper-tease-shout launches; integrate long-term by having them give away/sell your lead magnet or core offer.

**Rule of 100 → Open To Goal → Roadmap**: Rule of 100 daily actions per channel; elite operators work **open to goal** (work until X outcomes happen, whatever it takes). Seven-level roadmap: L1 friends know → L2 consistent inputs (warm + content) → L3 hire help → L4 referrals ≥25% of customers → L2 methods on multiple platforms → L5 executives → L7 $100M machine. Expect 3-6 months minimum to crack a NEW lead source even when experienced.
