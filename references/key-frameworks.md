## The Value Equation (Core Framework)

```
Value = (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood of Achievement)
 / (Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice)
```

**To increase perceived value:**
- **Increase** Dream Outcome — what's the best possible result?
- **Increase** Perceived Likelihood — make them believe they'll get it
- **Decrease** Time Delay — deliver faster
- **Decrease** Effort & Sacrifice — make it easier to buy

## Grand Slam Offer Formula

```
Irresistible Offer = Problem Agitation
 + Solution
 + Transformational Promise
 + Perceived Exclusivity
```

## Price Anchoring
Anchor to a high price first, then reveal the actual price. Makes the real price feel like a deal.

## The Reversal
Remove ALL risk from the customer. Guarantee satisfaction. "Design first, pay only after you approve."

## Value Stacking
List everything they get before revealing the price. The list of inclusions should feel worth more than the price.

## Assumptive Close
Act as if they've already decided. "When should I start?" not "Do you want to start?"

## Soft Exit
Leave the door open after a no. "No problem. Just reply anytime if you change your mind."
