# Chapter 18 — Make Your Money Model

## Core Idea
Assemble the four offer types into YOUR sequence, stage by stage. A Money Model that works makes one client's 30-day profit fund acquiring and servicing several more. Never build it all at once — perfect one offer at a time, then move to the next stage.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Three stages:** I Get Cash (Attraction) → II Get More Cash (Upsell+Downsell) → III Get The Most Cash (Continuity)
- **Make-Your-Own 4 steps:** pick Attraction → pick Upsell → pick Downsell → pick Continuity
- **Evolution ladder:** customers reliably → pay for themselves → pay for others → maximize LTV → pour ads on top

## Key Concepts
- Gym Launch's actual evolution: Decoy ($476k/month in 3 months) → Classic Upsell "Gym Lords" $42k/year + Payment Plan Downsell (~$1.5M/month) → Menu Upsell + Feature Downsells (~$2.3M/month) → integrated Prestige Labs ($4.4M/month by month 20).
- Raise price in stages: start low enough to get yeses, use feedback to improve, raise until extra yeses no longer cover lost nos.
- Simple scales, fancy fails: 100 ways to offer one product > 100 products.
- Affiliate products fill offer gaps with zero delivery load (local service agency referring braces manufacturer).
- Automatic renewal converts attraction offers into continuity (bulk term rolls into month-to-month).
- Mix and match freely — Upsell tactics inside attraction offers etc.; the section order is a default, not a law.
- When the model works, operations will break — build the team.

## Anti-patterns
- Launching a full Money Model day one (it collapses on you).
- Measuring in weeks instead of quarters; rebuilding instead of building right.

## Worked Example
Newsletter monster (Hormozi's favorite): Free trial ($0→$399/month after 30 days) + Pay Less Now/Pay More Later ("$297 now, keep that rate for life") = attraction + upsell + continuity in one six-headed offer.

**Local Agency full model (draft):**
- Stage I Attraction: Decoy — free homepage teardown call → DIY checklist vs done-for-you sprint; seasonal Giveaway for lead bursts.
- Stage II Upsell: Anchor pitch (a premium growth system) rescuing into Launch Sprint your base price + named bundles (Launch Package a package price); Menu close with card-on-file.
- Stage II Downsell: payment plan on paydays → feature tiers → trial-with-penalty last.
- Stage III Continuity: Care plan /4-weeks, bonus joining kit, waived your base price setup on 12-month commit, gift-card free month, lifetime rate at month 6.
- Test: does one client's 30-day gross (≈a blended monthly amount) fund 2+ more clients' CAC? If yes, spend on ads without fear.

## Key Takeaways
- Sequence > components. Stage-gate each piece until reliable.
- Price up relentlessly once the offer proves itself.
