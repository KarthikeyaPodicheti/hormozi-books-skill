# Chapter 13 — Trial With Penalty

## Core Idea
Last resort downsell: start free, but ONLY if they meet terms that make them a great customer (attend calls, submit homework). Miss the terms → fee. It's not "see if you like it" — it's structured usage with consequences. Changes what they pay TODAY, not in total.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Trial With Penalty**: free access conditioned on tracked actions; penalties per miss or lump; card + commitment captured upfront

## Key Concepts
- Reuse Win-Your-Money-Back criteria as trial criteria — completing it manufactures a perfect long-term customer.
- Sequence: offer main offer first; trial only after no.
- **Always get card (+ID) BEFORE explaining fees** ("that's just how we've always done it"); have them initial fee clauses separately.
- Sell staying FIRST: "if this gets you the result, will you stay long-term?" No answer = no trial.
- Prefer small per-miss fees over one lump fee ($50×10 misses vs $500 once).
- Required check-ins are mandatory = scheduled upsell moments.
- Post-trial paths: love it → upsell longer/bigger; hate it → take blame, roll into better-fit higher tier (~50% convert); didn't use it → waive fee if they meet you; don't bill non-starters over pocket change.
- Call it a "free trial," never a penalty. A $1 first month justifies taking the card more comfortably than $0.

## Anti-patterns
- Free trials with no card, no conditions, no commitment conversation (churn factory).
- Explaining penalties before capturing payment method (kills take rate).
- Punishing people with fees when a small waiver saves the relationship.

## Worked Example
Leila bought HR software because of one twist: complete onboarding training (3 Zoom calls + homework) and the $500 onboarding fee is waived; skip it and you pay. She did the training, learned the complex tool, and stayed — switching costs had quietly risen.

## Key Takeaways
- Converts the 40–70% who say no to paid offers without discounting anything.
- Money comes from conversions to long-term clients, not from fee collection.

**Example play:** "Can't do your base price? Start free: pay a small activation fee today; if you send your content by day 3 and approve designs within 48 hours each round, the remaining is waived when you join the /month care plan at launch."
