# Chapter 8 — The Classic Upsell

## Core Idea
Your first offer solves one problem and REVEALS the next one. Offer its solution immediately: "You can't have X without Y." Current customers buy far more readily than strangers; timed right, they upsell themselves.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Classic Upsell** — solution to the next problem at the moment of awareness

## Key Concepts
- Upsells fail three ways: wrong thing, wrong time, wrong delivery. All fixable by knowing the customer's problem better than they do.
- Get people to **say no to say yes**: "You don't want anything else, do you?" — trained reflex converts a no into agreement.
- **Hyper-buying cycle**: short window when buyers spend heavily (new local service business opening, new practice, renovation launch) — keep offering through it.
- Bonuses can CREATE upsell problems (free earmuffs needed $30 storage).
- Faster access = more perceived value; put it in their hands pre-yes if possible.
- Name bundles by outcome ("Transformation package") — one ask, many sales.
- **BAMFAM**: book-a-meeting-from-a-meeting; every appointment ends with the next one scheduled and why.
- Charge for guarantees/warranties/insurance (+5–50%) instead of giving them away.
- Lead with the higher-profit option when both solve the problem.

## Anti-patterns
- Selling exactly one thing forever.
- Offering the upsell before the client feels the problem.

## Worked Example
Burger shop: burger profit $0.25. +fries $0.75 → +drink $1.75 → supersize $1 → $3.00 total. An 11.6x profit increase from asking three questions. Without fries and soda there is no McDonald's.

## Key Takeaways
- Every purchase opens an upsell slot; map your problem-solution chain in advance.
- You lose nothing by offering; worst case is "no."

**Sample upsell ladder:** site launched → "you can't have a website patients find without Google Business Profile setup ()... and you can't convert visits without WhatsApp booking ()." End every handoff call booking the 30-day review call.
