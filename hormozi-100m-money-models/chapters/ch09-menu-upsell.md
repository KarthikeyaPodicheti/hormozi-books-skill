# Chapter 9 — Menu Upsell

## Core Idea
Stop asking "do you want to buy?" Ask "which one do you want?" Four tactics stacked: **Unsell → Prescribe → A/B choice → Card on file**. Buying becomes the path of least resistance.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Menu Upsell** combining: Unselling (cross out what they don't need), Prescription Upselling (tell them exactly what they need and how they'll use it), A/B Upsell (preference between two options), Card On File (use the card already saved)

## Key Concepts
- Unselling builds trust AND clears the deck for what you actually sell; keep items on the menu just to cross them out.
- Prescription = personalized instructions written as if they've already bought ("take two of these after lunch").
- A/B works on anything: quantity, start date, flavor, time slot, color, payment method, delivery speed. Either answer = a sale.
- Add a nudge when clients lack experience ("chocolate is my favorite"; "most local service businesses pick Tuesday launch").
- Card-on-file kills hidden friction (choosing a card, pulling it out, re-deciding).
- Sold out? Take payment, delay delivery, set expectations.
- Employees should be encouraged to help clients "game the system."

## Anti-patterns
- Yes/no purchase questions (invites no).
- Vague generic suggestions instead of prescriptions.
- Making people re-enter payment details.

## Worked Example
Hormozi's supplement shelf: 19 failed consults pitching science. Consult #20: he just asked "chocolate or vanilla?" — sold. Then he wrote per-product instructions on the order form and asked "use the card on file?" — sold again, without asking whether she wanted anything. Twenty straight closes followed. Later: "unselling" weight-gainer to a woman who didn't need it made her buy everything else happily.

## Key Takeaways
- Preference questions outsell permission questions.
- Personalized prescription + saved card = frictionless upsell.

**Example play:** at handoff: "You don't need the blog add-on yet — local service businesses don't. You DO need GBP setup and WhatsApp booking. Gallery photos: do you want us to source stock or edit yours? ...Same card as the sprint?"
