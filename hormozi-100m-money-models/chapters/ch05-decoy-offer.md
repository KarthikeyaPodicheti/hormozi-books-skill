# Chapter 5 — Decoy Offer

## Core Idea
Advertise a free/cheap stripped-down version. When leads engage, present it BESIDE a premium version loaded with support, personalization, and guarantees. The contrast sells the premium; the decoy catches everyone else so nobody is lost.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Decoy Offer**: (1) advertise the lesser version, (2) present both, emphasize premium

## Key Concepts
- Build the decoy by removing: components, personalization, guarantees, access.
- Advertise benefits/outcomes ("21-day transformation"), never feature lists.
- Permission question for premium-first pitch: "Are you here for free stuff or lasting results?" Most say results → go straight to premium.
- Make contrast HUGE; if premium isn't converting, widen the gap.
- Discounted offers get fewer leads but higher show rates than free ones — use discounts where no-shows are costly (local service businesses!).
- Four ways to state any discount: % off / amount off / free portion / total-package price. Test which converts.
- Assumed close: friendly, bored-by-how-often-people-buy energy.

## Anti-patterns
- A decoy so good it cannibalizes premium (keep it genuinely basic).
- Apologizing for the price or presenting premium half-heartedly — treat the anchor like a fake and so will they.
- Leading with features in ads.

## Worked Example
The $5 tanning pass: everyone thought 5 days was enough; staff explained "the turkey talk" (you can't cook a turkey in half the time at double heat), then credited the pass toward a $19.99/month unlimited membership. Later Hormozi's gym offered free DIY plan vs $399 "Ultimate" with coaching + guarantee — 8 of 10 took the $399.

## Key Takeaways
- You close everyone: premium or decoy, both are wins.
- The decoy exists to make premium obvious, not to be sold hard.

**Example play:** ad = "Free 5-point website checklist for your local service business." On the call: Option A free checklist + DIY vs Option B done-for-you sprint your base price with a 7-day launch guarantee + 2 revision rounds + GBP setup. Ask: "do you want to build it yourself or have leads finding you this week?"
