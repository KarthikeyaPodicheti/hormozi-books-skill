# Chapter 3 — Win Your Money Back

## Core Idea
Client pays full price upfront; if they hit your tracked goals/actions within the timeframe, they get it back (cash or store credit). They bet on themselves; you get upfront cash, higher commitment, better results, and free advertising.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Win Your Money Back** with three qualifying modes: **Results** (hit the outcome), **Actions** (do the process), **Actions + Results**

## Key Concepts
- Criteria must be: (1) easy to track, (2) likely to produce results, (3) advertise your business (posts, reviews, referrals).
- ~10% will claim refunds even when criteria are fair — only run this if your refund rate stays <5%; fix the product first otherwise.
- Apply won credit **spread over a longer/monthre expensive thing** ( win → USD500/month off × 12), not as free months upfront. Keeps skin in the game.
- Required check-in meetings = upsell moments (supplement offer at orientation, membership at check-ins).
- "Make everyone a winner" privately mid-program: credit their fee toward the next program whether or not they hit the goal.
- Store credit converts the same as cash — prefer credit.
- The money is NOT from people failing; it's from people succeeding and buying the next thing.

## Anti-patterns
- Complicated criteria nobody can verify.
- Handing back lump-sum cash/free months that end the paying relationship.
- Running it while refund rates are high.

## Worked Example
Danny's gym: hesitant prospect proposes "a front-end program price; if I hit my goal, I get it back — you use my results in marketing." He hit the goal, then spent the refund on MORE training, and his before/afters brought 13 referrals. Scaled industry-wide to industry-scale revenue.

## Key Takeaways
- Best offer where customers must put in continuous effort (fitness, learning, marketing — and yes, growing a practice).
- Upfront cash + lowered risk + results + referrals in one structure.
- Always have a next thing for winners to spend credit on.
