# Chapter 17 — Waived Fee Offer

## Core Idea
Month-to-month costs a big setup fee; commit 12 months and the fee is waived. Quit early inside the term → the fee comes due. Clients stay because LEAVING costs more than STAYING. The cleanest commitment device for service retainers.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Waived Fee Offer:** setup fee = 3–5x monthly rate on month-to-month; waived on 12-month+ commit; payable if broken early (initialed at signup)

## Key Concepts
- Fees get them to START (avoid it by committing) AND to STICK (cancel = pay it).
- Early in a term, quitting is expensive → they stay engaged; later, staying ≈ quitting cost → they ride it out. Both favor you.
- Justify the fee honestly: "setup costs us money; test us month-to-month and you cover it, commit and we cover it."
- Bigger fee → more commitments; smaller fee (1.5–3x) → more month-to-month upfront cash. Choose per cash needs.
- Fee dies once the commitment completes (equity).
- Works best for slow-payoff services — SEO, investing, weight loss... and local-business marketing like local service business websites.
- Spicy variant: donate early-cancellation fees to a cause the client HATES.
- Alarm: >5% trying to cancel early means the product needs work, not stronger handcuffs.

## Anti-patterns
- Tiny fees that don't influence behavior.
- Waiving with no paper trail (get explicit initialed acknowledgment of early-exit cost).
- Handcuffing people into something they hate — they stay angry and review-bomb.

## Worked Example
High-ticket legend's model: "month-to-month + -style setup fee, or 12 months with fee waived." At a small ad budget/month with a setup fee: Option A = $6,000 day one, leave anytime; Option B = a small ad budget/month ×12, exit early = owe $5,000. Most commit; almost none quit after month 8 since finishing beats paying.

**Example play:** Care plan month-to-month = /month + your base price setup (site migration, access config). Commit 12 months → setup waived. Cancel at month 5 → owe the your base price. At month 13, cancel free forever.

## Key Takeaways
- Converts "flexibility" from a churn risk into a priced feature.
- One structure delivers both acquisition lift and retention.
