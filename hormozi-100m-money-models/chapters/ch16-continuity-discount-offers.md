# Chapter 16 — Continuity Discount Offers

## Core Idea
Commit to a term, get time free. The trash-truck version of Buy X Get Y Free applied to recurring revenue. Four ways to apply the discount — each trades signup-rate against churn differently.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Continuity Discount**, four application modes:
 - **Up front** (free period first, term starts after) — most signups, most churn, delayed cash; needs enforceable contracts
 - **At the end** (earn your free months with on-time payments) — fewest signups, lowest churn
 - **Spread** (discount divided across term: 3 free months on a year = $50/month off ×12) — cash keeps flowing; offer lifetime lock-in if all payments made
 - **After first 1–2 payments** ("last month upfront"/activation fee, then bonus applies) — Hormozi's preferred balance

## Key Concepts
- **Bill every 4 weeks, not monthly**: 13 four-week cycles/year vs 12 months = +8.3% revenue, zero work; at 20% margins that's +41% annual profit. Highest-value-per-word note in the book.
- Extend the term rather than eating into it (pay 12, get 3 free = 15 total); feature-downsell shorter terms from there.
- Add "plus 3% processing fee," waivable if they give a second payment method on file — recovers failed-payment churn and adds 3% topline. Prefer ACH/auto-debit.
- Deliver free time as a physical gift card redeemable after ~3 payments — many forget, you keep full price; or they gift it = referral.
- **Lifetime discount at your average churn month**: "stay past month 4, rate drops forever" — engineered retention spike exactly where people quit (rice company example: 15% off after 5 straight months).
- Cancellation policy: cancel fee = discounts received (they revert to month-to-month economics). Make cancelling easy to find; run exit interviews, waive the fee for feedback — saves ~⅓ of would-be cancels.

## Anti-patterns
- Frontloading free time with high-churn products and no contract.
- Monthly-only billing and single-card dependency.

## Worked Example
Trash guy: "I'll haul your trash FREE for one year if you contract me for five paid years." Fronted a year, ate ramen, then cash flooded; sold the company years later. Simple offer, brutal execution.

## Key Takeaways
- Free time is cheaper than discounts and feels bigger.
- Billing cadence and payment redundancy are silent profit levers most agencies never touch.

**Example play:** Care plan " every 4 weeks; commit 6 months and month 7 is free (delivered as a gift card you can gift to another local service business)." Second payment method (UPI mandate + card) gets the 3% fee waived. Lifetime rate _LIFETIME kicks in at month 6 (your churn point).
