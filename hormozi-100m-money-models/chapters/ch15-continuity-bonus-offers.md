# Chapter 15 — Continuity Bonus Offers

## Core Idea
Get people to JOIN recurring plans by giving an awesome bonus today (value > first month's payment), stacking member-exclusive bonuses, then upselling prepaid blocks. Sell the bonus, not the membership.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Continuity Bonus Offer:** one-time joining bonus + ongoing member perks + prepaid-term upsell
- **Standalone vs membership pricing table** (see cheatsheet): standalone priced 1.33x → ~50% pick continuity; 1.66x → 60%; 2x → 70%; 2.33x → 80%; 2.66x → 90%

## Key Concepts
- Advertise the bonus ("get the a premium local SEO package FREE"), reveal membership after interest.
- Bonus types: more-of-same, complementary, upgrade.
- Bonuses should be things you already make/do anyway (onboarding, past newsletters, templates) with REALISTIC value anchors.
- Physical bonus on digital service / digital bonus on physical service = cheap perceived value.
- Titles as bonuses: Silver/Gold/Diamond at 3/6/12 months — customers introduce themselves by title.
- Anchor the bonus first ("it's USD8,000... want to know how to get it free?").
- Want cash instead? Sell the bonus standalone at 1.33–2.66x; half still pay extra just to avoid recurring billing.
- Bulk prepaid upsell: only 1 in 8 takers on "buy 5 get 1 free" lifts 30-day profit 50%.
- Trade commitment for bonuses: restrict big bonuses to 3/6/12-month commits (fewer sales, better clients).

## Anti-patterns
- Inflated fake bonus values (destroys trust).
- Unrelated bonuses attracting wrong people (free t-shirt for tech services).
- "Join my membership" as the headline.

## Worked Example
Gym owner stopped leading with the challenge: pitch became "the challenge is FREE when you become a member + members get VIP class times, tanning, events." Result per 100 leads: before 34 challenge signups → 17 convert to membership later; after 15 challenges + 40 straight-to-membership + 8 six-month prepaids. Membership sales tripled AND upfront cash grew.

**Example play:** "Launch Sprint includes the Practice Launch Kit free when you start the /month Care Plan today." Kit = GBP optimization + review-response templates + 3 social posts — stuff you already produce.

## Key Takeaways
- Bonus-first framing multiplies continuity conversion without discounting.
- Prepaid terms convert some of that recurring into upfront cash — do both.
