# Chapter 7 — Pay Less Now or Pay More Later

## Core Idea
Offer two doors: pay full price LATER (card on file, billed after they've experienced value, cancellable if conditions met) or pay a DISCOUNT NOW plus bonuses. Either way you win: card captured, risk removed, cash accelerated.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Pay Less Now or Pay More Later**: delayed payment + conditional guarantee vs immediate discount + bonuses

## Key Concepts
- The "pay later" door lets you advertise "free" while collecting the card on file.
- Pay-now discount range: 20–50% + bonuses exclusive to the now-option (e.g., recordings, 1:1 call, materials).
- Promise must be a clear yes/no measurable result within the billing window ("reading speed doubles in 3 hours").
- Conditional satisfaction guarantee: qualify only if they showed up / did the work. Track it.
- Tuning dial: too many choosing "later" → sweeten "now" (bigger discount/better bonuses); too many choosing "now" → reverse.
- Alarm: >10% cancel before billing = over-promised, weak guarantee conditions, or price too high.
- Recurring version: start now at a lower rate for good, or later at a higher ongoing rate.

## Anti-patterns
- Vague promises that invite disputes ("improved online presence").
- Explaining nothing and eating silent cancellations instead of tracking qualifying conditions.

## Worked Example
Speed-reading workshop: "$0 today, $297 billed tomorrow unless you cancel — must attend to qualify" OR "$97 today + exclusive recordings." Hormozi took door one; his reading speed doubled; he happily paid $297. Then upsold an 8-week program. Three offers in one event.

## Key Takeaways
- Removes all purchase risk without giving away margin — the discount is the price of speed.
- Works for one-time services AND recurring.

**Example play:** "Mockup free, launch billed in 7 days (cancel if you hate it)" vs "approve mockup today, your base price flat + free GBP setup." Most local service businesses take the sure cheaper thing TODAY — which is the point.
