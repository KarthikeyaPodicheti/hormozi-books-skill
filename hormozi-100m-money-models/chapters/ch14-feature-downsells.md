# Chapter 14 — Feature Downsells

## Core Idea
Lower price by REMOVING things, never by discounting the same thing. Take away a feature, drop the price proportionally more than the perceived loss, ask "how about now?" People only see a removed feature's value once it's gone — which often re-upsells them back to full price.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Feature Downsell formula:** remove something → lower price → "how about now?"
- Removal dimensions: quantity, quality, service-level features, entire features, DFY→DWY→DIY

## Key Concepts
- Remove highest-value features first, price drops a little → many flip back to original ("screw it, keep the guarantee"). Remove stuff they hate + big price cut → they take the downsell happily.
- Service-quality levers: response speed, availability windows, 1:1 vs group, live vs recorded, personalization, guarantee length/coverage/terms, expirations.
- Never negotiate price for identical scope — "business terrorists" get payment plans (want cheaper now) or feature downsells (want cheaper overall), never both-same-thing.
- Temperature check after two failed downsells (1–10 rule); alternate payment-plan ↔ feature downsells until very hard to refuse.
- End each revised offer with "Deal?" / "Fair enough?"
- Name tiers aspirationally (First/Business/Economy); cheapest = "The Minimum" (implies baseline).
- Downsell CURRENT clients using features they don't use before they cancel — these became Hormozi's second-highest-value customers.
- Barter discounts for advertising: reviews, video testimonials, public progress posts, referrals.
- Free orientations rescue total-refusers into DIY products (~all attendees bought).

## Anti-patterns
- Ad-hoc price cuts under pressure.
- Removing so much that the downsell can't deliver the core result.

## Worked Example
High-ticket seller cut close rate from 25%→75% in one quarter WITHOUT plans or discounts: on price objection he offered "keep the money-back guarantee, or lose it and pay less?" Result per 100 calls: 35 buy main offer (up from 25) + 40 take the downsell. More full-price buyers AND more total buyers.

## Key Takeaways
- The first downsell exists to make them reconsider the ORIGINAL offer.
- Guarantees are features — charge for them, and remove them as a downsell lever.

**Example play:** tiers — Complete your base price (5 pages, booking, GBP, guarantee, 2 revisions) / Standard _LOW (3 pages, template, email support) / Minimum (1-page site, client supplies all content). On objection: drop the guarantee first — most local service businesses pay full to keep it.
