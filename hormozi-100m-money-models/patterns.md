# Patterns — Money Model Plays for Local Agency

Decision patterns, not definitions. Situation → play.

## Pattern 1: Client says "too expensive" (your base price sprint)
1. Never cut price for same scope.
2. Payment plan on their PAYDAY ("when does your OPD/consulting income land?").
3. Still no → temperature check 1–10. ≥8: three payments → spread weekly. ≤7: feature downsell (drop pages, guarantee, booking).
4. Still no → trial-with-penalty with card + activation fee, terms = content deadlines + approval SLAs.

## Pattern 2: Need a burst of upfront cash
- Prepaid bulk to existing care-plan clients (cap at 10%): "buy 6 months of care, get 6 free" at current rate.
- Buy X Get Y Free reframe for new clients: " sprint + 6 months care FREE" (raise base price first).
- Winback rollover to clients dormant 6+ months: credit what they paid toward an annual plan priced ≥4x the credit.

## Pattern 3: Client unhappy / refund request
- Refund instantly if they insist (rule). Otherwise ROLLOVER first: "roll the your base investment into the custom tier / annual plan."
- Hate-it post-trial: take blame, offer better-fit higher tier (~50% convert).

## Pattern 4: Competitor's abandoned client
- Find via negative Google reviews; "credit whatever you paid them toward your rebuild with us."

## Pattern 5: Converting one-off sprinters into recurring
- At handoff (peak satisfaction), Menu close: unsell blog add-on → prescribe GBP+care plan → A/B (stock vs their photos) → same card on file.
- Bonus join incentive worth > first month (Launch Kit).
- Lifetime discount set AT your observed churn month.
- Waived setup fee (3–5x monthly) for 12-month commits.

## Pattern 6: Low show-up rate on consults
- Switch ads from FREE to small-price (discounted offers show up more) — matters double since local service businesses are busy.

## Pattern 7: Lead flood needed this month
- Giveaway: grand prize = premium build (anchor a premium value); everyone else gets partial scholarship; deadlines to enter/claim/use.

## Pattern 8: Price anchoring any conversation
- Always present a premium growth system before the sprint. Gasp expected. Rescue by asking which premium feature they don't care about.

## Pattern 9: Billing hygiene (silent profit)
- Bill every 4 weeks (+8.3%/year). Add waivable 3% processing fee to force second payment method. Auto-debit/UPI mandate preferred. Retry declines same day.

## Pattern 10: Scaling decision
- One client's 30-day gross profit ≥ CAC + delivery ×2? Yes → raise ad spend. No → fix Stage I/II before spending.
