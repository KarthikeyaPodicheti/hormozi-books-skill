# Cheatsheet — Numbers & Decision Rules (Local Agency)

## Payment-plan thresholds
| Trigger | Rule |
|---|---|
| Objection "can't afford it" | Half now / half on payday |
| Can't do half | Three payments, then evenly spread across milestones |
| Two failed downsells | Temperature check: ≥8/10 keep payment plans; ≤7 switch to feature downsell |
| Pay-in-full reward | Quote inflated price, prepay discount 20–33% off the quoted number |
| Charge dates | Client's payday(s); retry declined cards same day (~⅓ recover) |
| Health metric | Total closes ↑ while % paid-in-full stays FLAT |

## Discount & pricing rules
| Context | Number |
|---|---|
| Rollover upsell target price | ≥4x the credited amount (max 25% effective discount) |
| Giveaway promotional discount | 10–30% of gross margin |
| Pay-now discount (Pay Less Now) | 20–50% + bonuses |
| Cancel-before-billing alarm | >10% → over-promised / weak conditions / too pricey |
| Early-cancel alarm (waived fee) | >5% → fix product, not contract |
| Refund-rate ceiling for Win-Your-Money-Back | <5% or don't run it |
| Prepaid bulk cash pop | Offer to ≤10% of existing recurring clients |

## Continuity math (continuity plan, base /month)
| Goal (% choosing membership over standalone) | Standalone price multiple |
|---|---|
| 50% | 1.33x (≈a higher standalone equivalent) |
| 60% | 1.66x |
| 70% | 2x |
| 80% | 2.33x |
| 90% | 2.66x |

- Waived setup fee: 3–5x monthly rate (several times the monthly rate), minimum 12-month commit.
- Bill every 4 weeks: +8.3% revenue/year (≈+41% profit at 20% margins).
- Processing fee: +3%, waived for second payment method on file.
- Bulk prepaid upsell: a small take rate on "buy 5 get 1 free" lifts 30-day profit ~50%.

## Cash-flow decision table
| Situation | Play | Cash timing |
|---|---|---|
| New lead, cold | Decoy call → premium-first pitch | your base price on close |
| Price objection | Payday-split payment plan | 50–62% upfront |
| Hard objection | Feature downsell tiers | 50–70% of full |
| Almost lost | Trial w/ penalty + activation fee | a small activation fee now, rest contingent |
| Winner of WYMB challenge | Rollover into annual, credit spread | Pays from month 1 |
| Dormant client 6mo+ | Winback rollover credit | Annual prepay |
| Need cash THIS month | Bulk prepaid to top 10% of base | Immediate lump |
| Every handoff | Bundle upsell + care plan + prepaid term | +50–175% per client |

## Sequence checklist (build in order)
- [ ] Stage I: attraction offer gets customers who cover its own cost
- [ ] Stage II: upsell ladder + downsell tree push 30-day profit past 2× (CAC+delivery)
- [ ] Stage III: continuity stacks recurring; prepaid terms pull cash forward
- [ ] Only then: increase ad spend
