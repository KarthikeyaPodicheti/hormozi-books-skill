# Patterns & Decision Rules — $100M Offers (Local Agency edition)

Decision rules, thresholds, and trade-offs. Not definitions — see SKILL.md and chapters/ for concepts.

## Pricing decisions
- IF a prospect can name a cheaper twin of your offer AND pick it → you're commoditized → restructure the OFFER (stack, guarantee, niche callout), never cut price.
- Raise price ONLY after adding perceivable value; every raise should still leave the buyer feeling they won. Sequence: value ↑ → price ↑ → repeat.
- Price against outcome value (local service business's patient LTV, designer's project margin), not against other freelancers. If you can't say the price out loud without a smile, either fix conviction or fix the stack.
- Discounting is allowed only on non-core levers: bonuses, urgency windows, payment timing. Core price is never negotiated.
- Niching threshold: under ~$10M/year revenue, narrowing always beats broadening. One avatar per offer; separate offers per avatar if needed, not one blended one.

## Offer construction
- Walk the customer journey end-to-end; every objection heard on a sales call becomes a permanent named deliverable within 1 week. Rule: no objection dies twice.
- Prefer one-to-many assets (templates, checklists, swipe files) over 1-on-1 time at equal perceived value; reserve 1-on-1 for premium tiers only.
- Trim order when cutting cost: high-cost/low-value first, then low-cost/low-value. Never trim high-value items without raising price to compensate.
- Stated bonus value should exceed core offer value (~2-3x is typical); attach and justify a number to EVERY component.
- If fulfillment hurts but sales are easy → systemize delivery before touching the offer. If sales are hard but fulfillment is easy → add stack/guarantee/naming before touching delivery.

## Scarcity & urgency
- Always undersupply: open fewer slots than you think you can sell, then announce the sell-out. Sell-out announcements are mandatory, not optional.
- Publish real capacity % ("3 of 4 sprint slots booked") weekly. Fake scarcity once = permanent credibility tax.
- Urgency anchors to promotions/bonuses/dates, NEVER to service availability you can't enforce.
- Deadline power curve: expect 50-60% of responses in the final hours — don't panic-promote mid-window; do remind hard in the last 24h.
- Announce price rises 1-2 weeks ahead to flush the pipeline; never raise silently.
- Cadence sweet spot for limited releases/promos: monthly. More often dilutes; less often loses momentum.

## Guarantees
- Run the math before any guarantee: net gain = extra closes − incremental refunds. A guarantee fails only if refund-rate increase fully offsets close-rate increase (rare).
- Ticket × type matrix:
 - Low ticket + B2C → unconditional no-questions.
 - Mid ticket / B2B services → conditional tied to client actions, or delayed-payment ("don't bill until first outcome").
 - High ticket + high fulfillment cost → service guarantee (work free until result) or anti-guarantee with a bold reason why.
- Conditions must map to actions that actually drive success (client approves content in 48h, provides photos by day 2). This filters guarantee-hunters and improves outcomes.
- Stack: short unconditional window + longer conditional outcome window future-paces belief.
- Track refunds as data: spike after a guarantee change → tighten conditions or fix delivery, don't remove risk reversal wholesale.

## Naming & fatigue
- Vary in this order only: creative → copy → name/wrapper → duration → free/discount enhancer → monetization structure. Touching lower layers while upper ones still work wastes ops effort.
- Local/hyper-niche markets fatigue fastest — rotate wrapper monthly; keep the bundle identical underneath.
- Test 2-3 M-A-G-I-C names against each other; winner becomes control until beaten.

## Local Agency quick applications
- Sprint slots: cap at true weekly capacity, publish fill %, sell out, announce it.
- Stack sketch: core sprint + DFY copy + photo checklist + Google Business Profile + WhatsApp booking + review templates + 30-day tweak window, each named and priced.
- Guarantee ladder: start with "live in 7 days or we keep working free" (service guarantee); graduate to performance-flavored retainers once track record exists.
- Partner bonuses: local service suppliers, print labs, local photographers — their product as your bonus + referral commission back to you.
