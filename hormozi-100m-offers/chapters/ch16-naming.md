# Chapter 16: Enhancing The Offer — Naming

## Core Idea
The name is wrapping paper: same offer, different wrapper, 2x-10x response. Offers fatigue (fastest in local markets), so generate unlimited names with the M-A-G-I-C formula and vary in a fixed order without touching what works underneath.

## Frameworks Introduced
- M-A-G-I-C naming formula
- Offer-fatigue variation ladder

## Key Concepts
- **M — Magnetic reason-why**: Free, 88% off, Grand Opening, Anniversary, New Year, Back-to-School — answers "why this deal exists?" Can be nearly anything if you believe it (the wisdom-teeth party).
- **A — Avatar**: call out exactly who it's for; hyper-local beats city ("Towson moms," "Lakeway local service businesses").
- **G — Goal**: the dream outcome, tangible ("Celebrity Smile," "Fill Your Gym," "Double Your Profit").
- **I — Interval**: duration ("21-Day," "6-Week"); note platforms may reject quantifiable claims paired with durations.
- **C — Container word**: Challenge, Blueprint, Bootcamp, Sprint, Accelerator, Intensive, Reset — signals an uncomparable system/bundle.
- Use 3-5 components, any order, punchy > complete; rhyme/alliteration are nice-to-have. Test 2-3 names against each other; keep the winner as control.
- Name sub-items and bonuses with the same formula — instant value lift.
- Fatigue variation order: creative → body copy → headline/wrapper → duration → enhancer (free/discount component) → monetization structure. Exhaust light levers before heavy ones; once monetized, rarely change the machine.
- Local marketing paradox: easier to convert (trust in familiarity) but fatigues faster (small TAM radius) — rapid wrapper rotation is THE local skill.

## Anti-patterns
- Lazy generic names ("Website Design Package") that commoditize instantly.
- Changing the actual offer/pricing when only creative fatigue was the problem — entrepreneurial ADD creates operational drag.
- Forcing rhyme/alliteration until the name gets weird.

## Worked Examples
Wellness: "Free Six-Week Lean-By-Halloween Challenge"; doctors: "Lakeway Moms — $1,500 Off Your Kids Braces," "Back Sore No More! 90 Day Rapid Healing Intensive (81% off)"; coaching: "5 Clients in 5 Days Blueprint," "Fill Your Gym in 30 Days (Free!)".

## Key Takeaways
- Local Local service examples: "Dentist Double-Booking Sprint — Live Website in 7 Days," "Interior Studio Client-Magnet Makeover (4 slots/monthnth)," "Free 5-Day Website Rescue Challenge for your local market Dentists."
- Rotate wrappers seasonally; never touch the converting bundle beneath.
