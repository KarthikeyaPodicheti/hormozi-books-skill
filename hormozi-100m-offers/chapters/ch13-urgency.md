# Chapter 13: Enhancing The Offer — Urgency

## Core Idea
Urgency limits TIME, not quantity. Deadlines drive decisions. Four ethical mechanisms let even always-open businesses use urgency honestly.

## Frameworks Introduced
- Cohort-Based Rolling Urgency
- Rolling Seasonal Urgency
- Pricing/Bonus-Based Urgency
- Exploding Opportunity

## Key Concepts
- **Cohort rolling urgency**: "Sign today → start Monday's kickoff; otherwise wait for the next." Less frequent kickoffs = more power. If someone misses the cutoff: either a paid-speed onboarding bonus to catch up, or reframe waiting as an advantage (more prep time + extended payment plan only available to them). You always control the frame.
- **Rolling seasonal urgency**: same promo re-wrapped with real dates each month/season ("New Year Promo ends Jan 30" → "Valentines Promo..."). Dates must be real. #1 strategy for local businesses — novelty without operational change.
- **Pricing/bonus-based urgency**: the DISCOUNT or BONUS expires, never the service itself — preserves integrity while creating the same FOMO ("this bonus goes away Friday; we rotate promos every 4 weeks").
- **Exploding opportunity**: when the underlying opportunity genuinely decays (arbitrage gaps, new ad platforms, early-mover advantages), emphasize the decay.
- Deadline math: 50-60% of launch sales occur in the last 4 hours of the last day — 3% of the timeline producing most of the revenue. People who "miss out" were never going to buy anyway.
- Price-rise announcements clean the pipeline: "price goes up on X" flushes fence-sitters and signals strength. Never raise prices silently.

## Anti-patterns
- Fake evergreen countdown timers that reset — credibility once lost doesn't return.
- Claiming the service disappears after Friday when it obviously won't; anchor urgency to the promotion instead.

## Worked Example
Seasonal rotation for a local business: identical core offer renamed monthly with hard end-dates on landing pages and ads; conversions "go through the roof" from ~5 minutes of editing per cycle.

## Key Takeaways
- Local Agency: monthly themed sprint windows ("Diwali Booking-Boost Sprint — closes Oct 15"), cohort-style kickoff dates, and announced price increases ahead of time.
- Put real visible dates everywhere the offer appears; deadlines do the closing for you.
