# Chapter 7: Free Goodwill

## Core Idea
Give value away with zero expectation. It compounds: goodwill, trust, reach — and it's the engine behind Hormozi's free-books-for-equity-clients model. Practically: deliver value in advance to earn attention and trust before asking for money.

## Frameworks Introduced
- Goodwill as a business strategy (value-first marketing)

## Key Concepts
- People who help others with zero expectation live longer and make more money.
- Introducing something valuable associates that value with YOU — forwarding the book is goodwill generation.
- The chapter doubles as a live demonstration: an ask framed entirely around what the reader gets and who it helps.

## Anti-patterns
- Hoarding every insight behind paywalls when free distribution would build the audience that buys everything else.
- Giving with strings attached — recipients feel the transaction and goodwill dies.

## Worked Example
The book itself: given away at cost, plus free courses/SOPs/checklists at Acquisition.com, converting readers into trust-based buyers and eventually equity-investment candidates ($3M-$10M businesses).

## Key Takeaways
- For Local Agency: free teardown videos of a prospect's current website, free "5 things costing your local service business bookings" audits — value in advance beats cold pitches.
- Every piece of free value should be genuinely useful AND demonstrate capability (proof of Likelihood).
