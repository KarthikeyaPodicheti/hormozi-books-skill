# Chapter 9: Creating Your Grand Slam Offer Part I — Problems & Solutions

## Core Idea
Steps 1-3 of offer creation: (1) name the Dream Outcome, (2) exhaustively list every problem the prospect will hit before/during/after using your service, (3) convert each problem into a solution statement. Solve EVERY problem — one unsolved objection can kill the sale.

## Frameworks Introduced
- 5-Step Offer Creation Process (steps 1-3)
- Four problem buckets (mirroring the Value Equation)

## Key Concepts
- Sell the vacation, not the flight: Hormozi stopped selling a $99/month membership and sold "Lose 20lbs in 6 Weeks."
- Problem listing = walk the customer's entire journey in insane detail; ask what they need immediately before and after using your product ("what's the NEXT thing they need?").
- Four buckets per obstacle: (1) worth it financially? (2) will it work for ME specifically? (most unique bucket), (3) too hard/confusing/I'll suck at it? (4) too long/too inconvenient?
- 16 core problems × 2-4 sub-problems = 32-64 problems. More problems found = more value creatable.
- Solution conversion is copywriting 101: "How to [buy healthy food] easily and enjoyably so even busy moms can do it."
- Brooke Castillo's 90-Day Relationship course used the identical process — industry-agnostic.

## Anti-patterns
- Insisting prospects adapt to YOUR method instead of solving their stated obstacle (the eating-out story — he lost sales for months until he made an eating-out guide; that one template removed the objection forever).
- Listing only 3-4 obvious problems and calling the exercise done.
- Getting fancy with wording instead of just getting every problem down.

## Worked Example
Weight loss offer skeleton: Dream outcome = lose 20 lbs in 6 weeks → problems across buying/cooking/eating/exercise, each with 4+ objections → solutions list like "How to buy healthy food for less than your current grocery bill," "How to cook meals in under 5 minutes."

## Key Takeaways
- Local Local service version: Dream Outcome = "a booking-ready website in 7 days." Problems to enumerate: local service business doesn't know what content to write, no photos, fears looking cheap, doesn't understand domains/hosting, worried about Google ranking, has no time for calls, scared of maintenance costs, etc. Each becomes a named solution in our stack.
- Every objection heard on a sales call should become a permanent solved item (template/guide/bonus).
