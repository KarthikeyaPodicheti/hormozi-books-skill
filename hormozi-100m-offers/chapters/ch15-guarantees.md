# Chapter 15: Enhancing The Offer — Guarantees

## Core Idea
Risk is the #1 objection; reversing it can 2-4x conversion (Fladlien). Spend disproportionate time crafting the guarantee. Structure: "If you don't get X result in Y time, we will Z" — no "or what," no teeth.

## Frameworks Introduced
- Four guarantee types: Unconditional, Conditional, Anti-Guarantee, Implied
- Guarantee math
- Stacking guarantees

## Key Concepts
- Math first: 100 sales @5% refunds = 95 net; guarantee-driven 130 sales @10% = 117 net → +23%. Refunds must nearly double relative to sales lift before a guarantee loses money.
- **Unconditional**: pay-first trial; strongest, best for low-ticket/B2C; requires delivery confidence. Fladlien's wording: "I'm not asking you to decide yes or no today... I'm asking you to make a fully informed decision."
- **Conditional** (most creative space): Outsized refund (double/triple back — Fladlien's "$25k store buyout" generated $3M extra sales); Service guarantee (work free until result — Hormozi's favorite; nobody ever claims it); Modified service (double the term free); Credit-based (upsells); Personal service guarantee (1-on-1 until result, heavily conditioned); Hotel+airfare reimbursement; Wage-payment guarantee (nobody ever collects); Release of contract; Delayed second payment (don't bill till first outcome — great when you know your activation metric); First outcome (we cover your costs till first sale).
- **Anti-guarantee**: "all sales final" + bold vulnerable reason why (once seen, can't be unseen; we expose our inner process) — filters for committed buyers; works for high-customization/high-cost fulfillment.
- **Implied**: performance/revshare/profit-share/ratchets/triggers — "if you don't win, I don't get paid"; most aligned; needs trackable outcomes and collection trust. Agencies moving retainers→performance went $20k/month→$200k+/month. Pair with minimums ("greater of $X or Y%") during ramp-up.
- Conditions should require the actions that actually drive success — converts guarantee-hunters into compliant clients. Given refund vs outcome, almost everyone takes the outcome.
- Stack guarantees: unconditional short-window + conditional long-window future-paces belief.
- High-ticket/high-cost services → conditional or anti-guarantees (you eat fulfillment cost AND refund otherwise).

## Anti-patterns
- Vanilla "30-day satisfaction guarantee" wording — no imagery, no teeth.
- Blanket unconditional guarantees on expensive custom work.
- Using guarantees to patch a bad product ("guarantees enhance; they cannot make a business").

## Worked Example
Weight-loss close: "Best case you hit your goal; worst case you tell me I suck, I write you a check, you got six weeks of free training. Both options are risk-free." Two refunds in 4,000 sales over 3.5 years.

## Key Takeaways
- Local Agency candidates: Service guarantee ("site launches in 7 days or we keep working free"), delayed second payment for retainers ("don't pay month 2 until your site has produced X inquiries"), or anti-guarantee framing for fully custom builds.
- Name it memorably; state conditions tied to client homework (content approval within 48h etc.).
