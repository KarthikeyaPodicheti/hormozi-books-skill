# Chapter 1: How We Got Here

## Core Idea
The offer is the starting point of every transaction and the lifeblood of a business. No offer = no business; bad offer = negative profit; Grand Slam Offer = freedom. Hormozi's Gym Launch nearly died on Christmas Eve 2016 (partner stole $46k, payment processor froze $120k) and survived purely because he had a Grand Slam Offer that generated $100k in its first month.

## Frameworks Introduced
- The offer ladder: commodity → decent → good → Grand Slam Offer
- Business as value exchange initiated by an offer

## Key Concepts
- Entrepreneurs' two universal problems: not enough clients, not enough cash — both stem from weak offers.
- Implementing a new offer is the fastest lever in a business (no ops/culture overhaul needed).
- Skill in offer-making turns acquisition from gambling into being the house.
- "It's better to understand why you failed than be ignorant of why you succeeded" — frameworks make luck repeatable.

## Anti-patterns
- Treating marketing/sales problems as traffic problems when the offer itself is weak.
- Copying business models built by funded companies that can run at losses for years ("buying yourself a job").
- Blaming yourself when the offer fails — "if your offer doesn't work, it means your OFFER sucks."

## Worked Example
Gym Launch, Jan 2017: $3,300/day burning on credit card across six gym launches. The challenge-offer methodology produced $100,117 in month one, ~$3M profit in year one, $1.5M+/month by year end. One Grand Slam Offer carried everything.

## Key Takeaways
- Fix the offer before fixing anything else.
- A failed attempt is data, not identity — iterate offers relentlessly.
- You only need to hit one Grand Slam Offer to change your trajectory permanently.
