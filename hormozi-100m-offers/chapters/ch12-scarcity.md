# Chapter 12: Enhancing The Offer — Scarcity

## Core Idea
Scarcity limits QUANTITY. Fixed or shrinking supply creates FOMO, which forces action and raises price. Fear of loss beats desire for gain.

## Frameworks Introduced
- Three types of scarcity: limited seats/slots, limited bonuses, never-available-again
- Service scarcity variants: Total Business Cap, Growth Rate Cap, Cohort Cap
- Honest Scarcity

## Key Concepts
- Authority pricing (doctors, celebrities charging egregious rates) is implicit scarcity — assumed huge demand, tiny supply of their time.
- Specialized problems have Supply=1 solvers; rarity × value compounds into breathtaking prices ($50k/day consults).
- Physical products: limited releases work ONLY if you always sell out — better to sell out than over-supply; announce the sell-out (social proof + stored desire). Monthly cadence is the sweet spot. Chanel ships 1-2 pieces per store per design for exactly this reason.
- Services tactics:
 1. **Total Business Cap** — "only 25 clients ever at this tier"; waiting list kills price resistance when a slot opens; squeeze out low-margin accounts over time.
 2. **Growth Rate Cap** — "5 new clients/week, 3 spots already taken"; matches real onboarding capacity.
 3. **Cohort Cap** — X clients per start date; builds operational cadence AND legitimate scarcity.
- Honest scarcity: publish your true capacity and % full ("81% to capacity"). You choose where "full" is; social proof accelerates fill.
- Extreme scarcity play: tiny-capped 1-on-1 access tier priced very high — unscalable but hugely profitable, attracts the best clients.
- Once-you're-out-you-can't-return caps add teeth to retention (works best for small groups).

## Anti-patterns
- Claiming scarcity you visibly violate (fake countdown timers).
- Over-ordering/over-opening slots so scarcity never actually triggers.
- Using scarcity with zero social proof to back it up.

## Worked Example
Free checklist made scarce: only 20 downloads allowed per week. When it runs out, visitors join a waitlist and rush the moment it reopens — a "neat free download" became a contested resource purely via supply control.

## Key Takeaways
- Local Agency: "We take 4 website sprints per month. 3 booked." True capacity framing, updated weekly.
- Announce sell-outs publicly ("March sprints fully booked") — cheapest social proof available.
- Reserve a tiny ultra-premium tier (e.g., 1 concierge sprint/month with direct access) priced several times standard.
